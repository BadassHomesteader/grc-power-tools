Power Tools

The PowerToys of macOS.

One hotkey, a whole toolbox — dictation, AI chat, window snapping, Advanced Paste, OCR and more. The core tools never leave your Mac.

Hold Option + Shift and talk — release, and polished text lands in whatever app has focus.

Everything behind one key

Hold your hotkey, then speak or tap a letter/arrow. If you've missed Windows PowerToys on the Mac, this is that — plus dictation and AI.

🎙

Dictation

Hold, speak, release. On-device transcription with LLM cleanup — no cloud.

💬

AI chat

hold + A then speak — opens a streaming Claude chat with your message.

🪟

Window snapping

hold + ←→↑↓ to snap; tap again to cycle ½ · ⅓ · ⅔. Plus Snap Assist.

▦

Grid placement

hold + 3 and drag across a grid to place a window, Moom-style.

📋

Advanced Paste

hold + P to paste as plain text — or summarize, rewrite, bullets, translate.

🔤

OCR / Text Extractor

hold + T and drag a region — recognized text (and tables) to your clipboard.

🗂

File cut & paste

hold + C / X / V to copy, cut, and move files in Finder from the keyboard.

📸

Screenshots & Lens

hold + S to grab a region; hold + G for Google Lens.

🖱

Find My Mouse

hold + M dims the screen and spotlights your cursor.

Local by default

Dictation never leaves the machine — speech recognition is Apple's on-device SpeechAnalyzer, cleanup is Apple's on-device model, and history lives in a local SQLite file.

The only network calls are the opt-in AI features — AI chat, cloud cleanup, and Advanced Paste's smart transforms — and only when you use them, with your own API key. Little-Snitch-clean by design.

Install in a minute

No App Store, no account. Download, drag, grant a couple of permissions.

1

Download & open

Grab the .dmg and drag Power Tools into Applications. It's signed & notarized, so it just opens.

2

Grant permissions

Allow Microphone and Accessibility when prompted — needed for the hotkey and dictation.

3

Hold ⌥⇧ and go

Hold Option + Shift and speak, or tap a letter/arrow for any of the tools.
